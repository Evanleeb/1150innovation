The offering

A new era of industrial ownership in Boca Raton.

Twenty industrial flex condominiums in two new rear-load buildings in the Park of Broken Sound, on one of the last developable sites inside Boca Raton's established industrial park. Each unit runs approximately 3,581 square feet, with 3,031 square feet on the ground floor and a 550 square foot mezzanine over the office. Buy one, or combine adjacent units for a larger footprint.

The buildings face one another across a shared interior court, so loading is concealed from the street and every unit presents a glass storefront and its own entry to landscaped parking. At 2.7 spaces per 1,000 square feet, the site parks well above a typical industrial condominium, which is what makes real office and showroom use possible inside the unit rather than a token front room.

Rent is compared on a triple net basis, so taxes, insurance and common area costs are excluded from both sides since an owner and a tenant each bear them. Ownership figures exclude condominium assessments, closing costs and tenant improvements. Rent is treated as fully deductible. The blended rate approximates a conventional first mortgage combined with an SBA 504 debenture. Depreciation assumes a 39 year recovery period on the building shell, with a cost segregation study reclassifying a portion to short life property eligible for 100% bonus depreciation in year one under current law; the actual amount depends on an engineering study and is not known in advance. Depreciation deductions require sufficient taxable income to absorb them, may be limited by passive activity, basis and at risk rules, and are recaptured on sale. Appreciation is an input you control, not a forecast; set it to zero to see the result without it. The case for ownership

10 reasons to own

  • 01
    Turn rent into equityTen years of rent leaves you with nothing. The same decade of payments leaves you owning the building.
  • 02
    As little as 10% downSBA 504 may be available to qualified owner-occupants, fixing 40% of the debt for 25 years.
  • 03
    Fix your occupancy cost permanentlyNo renewal negotiations, no market resets, no annual escalations you do not control.
  • 04
    Capture the appreciation yourselfBoca Raton industrial has no land supply left. Value accrues to the owner, not the tenant.
  • 05
    Deduct interest and depreciate the assetInterest is deductible, and a cost segregation study can pull a large deduction into year one.
  • 06
    Build the space around your operationSpecify your own power, finishes and buildout. No landlord consent, no restoration.
  • 07
    Buy for where you are goingAdjacent units can be combined at purchase, so you size today against tomorrow.
  • 08
    Never move againLocation stability protects your workforce, your customers, and the goodwill in your address.
  • 09
    A park of owners, not tenantsOwner-occupants maintain their buildings and hold the standard of the park.
  • 10
    A transferable, financeable assetSell it, hold it as retirement income, or lease it to your successor.
